## 从记录到发布

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  A[写下想法] --> B[整理文章]
  B --> C[本地预览]
  C --> D[静态构建]
  D --> E[GitHub Pages]
```

## 三个小步骤

```infographic
infographic list-row-simple-horizontal-arrow

    - label 回顾
```
